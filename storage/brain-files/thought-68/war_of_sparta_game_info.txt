WAR OF SPARTA — GAME INFORMATION SHEET

--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. BASIC INFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Title (EN):            War of Sparta
Title (AR):            حرب سبارتا: انتقام الأبطال
                       (also styled "حرب سبارتا: انتقام الملوك")
                       Literal EN: "War of Sparta: Revenge of the Heroes"
Genre:                 Strategy — MMO Real-Time Strategy (SLG / 4X);
                       multiplayer, competitive multiplayer & single
                       player modes
Setting / Theme:       Ancient Greece / Spartan mythology; war against
                       the Persian Empire
Languages:             Arabic, English
Platforms:             Android, iOS, Windows

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ABOUT THE GAME
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Amidst the endless clash of steel, you emerge as a Spartan leader
who knows no fear. Your mission: forge Sparta into an unbreakable
fortress, and train an army of warriors who never retreat. Form your
iron phalanx, defy the might of the Persian Empire, and let the name
of Sparta strike terror into the hearts of your enemies.
"Glory or death — this is our law!"

War of Sparta is a real strategy experience blending intelligence,
planning and heroism, set in a world inspired by Greek myth and
Spartan glory. Players build a kingdom, train heroes, and take part
in epic real-time battles displayed live on a world map.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. KEY GAME FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Core pillars:
  • Strategy
  • Build Your Senate (مجلس الشيوخ) — base / city building
  • Attack & Conquer (الهجوم والسيطرة)
  • Unlock Heroes / Leaders (القادة)

Feature highlights:
  • Real-time strategic battles — watch battles unfold live on the
    map; command armies and heroes in the moment to turn the tide.
    Manual or automatic control supported.
  • Heroes & legends — diverse hero system with legendary characters,
    each with unique abilities enabling endless strategy.
  • Alliances & loyalty — form your own alliance or join powerful
    kingdoms to control Mount Olympus; alliances are the core of
    power and competition.
  • Free resources & steady progress — earn diamonds and daily
    rewards from special events; accessible reward system.
  • Simple, engaging gameplay — smooth UI and streamlined design for
    beginners and veterans alike.
  • VIP membership — unique perks and special packages to help you
    climb to the top of the kingdom.
  • Fully Arabic experience — Arabic support, seasonal events tied to
    Arab occasions, and the ability to decorate your city with your
    country's flag.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. HEROES / CHARACTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Named legendary heroes: Leonidas, Athena, Hercules, Spartacus.
Each hero has unique abilities that unlock different strategies.
Heroes lead the troops and fight for you ("Heroes Allegiance"),
and can be leveled up to unlock new abilities and enhancements.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
5. GAMEPLAY & CORE MECHANICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Core loop:
  • Build and develop your own Spartan city.
  • Train troops and level up both heroes and units, unlocking new
    abilities and enhancements as you progress.
  • Defend your base against enemy attacks while planning raids —
    success requires balancing offense and defense.
  • Manage and gather resources to fund upgrades and research.

Real-time battles:
  • Battles play out live on the world map (a distinguishing feature
    vs. typical strategy games) — you can watch and command in the
    moment with manual or automatic control.

Progression & systems:
  • Key pillars: Resources, Upgrades, Heroes, and Leaderboard.
  • Building, research and upgrades run on timers; higher-tier items
    take longer and cost more materials (deep late-game grind).
  • An action-points / energy system paces continuous play.

Territory control:
  • Temple and Olympus Hill offer a way to occupy and hold a kingdom.
  • Alliances compete for control of Mount Olympus.

Alliances & multiplayer:
  • Form or join an alliance to wage large-scale wars.
  • Receive monster boxes and cooperative rewards.
  • Teleport your city closer to allies for mutual protection.

Events & economy:
  • Free-to-play with in-app purchases (freemium) and a VIP tier.
  • Frequent events award diamonds and daily rewards; seasonal
    events are tied to Arab occasions, and cities can be decorated
    with a player's country flag.
